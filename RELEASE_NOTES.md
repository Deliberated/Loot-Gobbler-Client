v0.0.3: Forge, focus and optimization

**ST Sets:** 
- Click a set to open a bar below it: every copy of its pieces, seasonal and regular, as the item beside the character or chest it sits in
- Missing pieces the forge can make show the tokens needed / held per side and the spare ST item to dismantle; "CRAFTABLE" when everything is there
- Click CRAFTABLE to plan the craft: its tokens and spare are set aside, so other sets no longer count them (until the app closes)
- Click any other item in the bar to see it on a card of its own (enchantments, where it is, feed power, essence, all copies); drag it anywhere
- Collector Mode switch: off, collector (best copy kept, gold lock) or collector + backup (second best kept too, green lock you can let go)
- The Forge column shows what each missing piece still needs and where your tokens are
- Filters reduced to All and Tokens; a focused character shows its class's sets only

**Items and ST Sets:** 
- Click ST Sets while on Items and the two melt into one page: your collection on the left, the sets on the right
- Click a set there and its pieces beat in the collection, everything else dimmed
- Gold pours between the tabs as they melt and part

**Inventory optimization:** 
- "Suggest Inventory Optimization" on the Overview, or the new button beside the collection's search
- Your spare copies are framed in the collection and listed on the left, with where each copy is and its feed power
- Feed sorts by feed power, Forge by what dismantling gives, Craft brings in the ST sets

**Inventory:** 
- A Keys page: every dungeon and mystery key you hold, regular and seasonal apart, and what each opens
- Vault places shown by their own chest: vault, seasonal vault, gift chest, seasonal spoils, material chest
- The column lists your focused characters with what they found while you played them

**Focus:** 
- Focus on any character with the eye on its Characters row
- The focus boxes stand on the line under the tabs; drag them to reorder

**Profile:** 
- The fame graph shows every run as its own portal; runs at the same time stack and open up on hover
- Overview boxes for what you can do next

**Battles and DPS:** 
- Boss fights show the main boss as a small table: you and the places around you
- POTENTIAL CHEATER marks players with real damage who took almost none
- The Third Dimension's cube blocks show as one boss

**Overlays:** 
- See-through on Windows with soft edges, no frame or shadow; the opacity slider no longer flickers
- The dungeon overlay can show the time in the dungeon; ticks choose time, rank and procs
- Dragging keeps the overlay under the mouse

**Fixes:** 
- Minimized, the app keeps up with the game instead of freezing when shown again
- Opening the app a second time brings up the running window instead of overwriting its records
- Your focused character's loot shows in the inventory
- No suggested loadout for characters in the graveyard
- The status bar says "rust stable"
