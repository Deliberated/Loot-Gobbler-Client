v0.0.4: Stats, swooshes and a steadier sync

**Character stats:** 
- Hover a character's portrait, name or x/8 badge in Characters to see every stat: its base value, the potions still needed to max it, and its exaltation bonus
- Maxed stats show in yellow; HP and MP count potions, which give 5 each
- While you play the character, its gear bonus shows too
- Stats are read from the character list saved at your last login, so no refresh is needed

**Exaltation follows your focus:** 
- With a character in focus, the Profile's Exaltation box shows its class: outlined in the strip, its exaltations so far and each stat's level out of 5

**Inventory optimization:** 
- Clicking Feed or Forge shows your spare copies right away
- Forge lists the rarities you left out for crafting, the worst copies first; the rarity button now reads "Considered for crafting"
- ST set cards that are not complete fade into the bar

**Animations:** 
- Switching sections pours a gold drop from the old button into the new one, through any buttons in between
- Pages slide in from the side of the tab or section you picked, with a gold swoosh
- Switching tabs briefly flashes a big icon of the tab

**Server sync:** 
- Fixed the sync stopping for good when an answer from the server never arrived; it now retries, so your drops reach the guild's Loot Caravan and Recent Drops again
