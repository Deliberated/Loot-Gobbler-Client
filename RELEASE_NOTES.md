v0.0.8: 

**Loot Caravan:** 
- Drops you just uploaded show on your wagon right away, instead of up to a minute later

**Themed Sets:** 
- A new Inventory page next to ST Sets: the 17 themed sets, four untiered items around one look and class each
- Built like the ST Sets: your progress per set, and a click opens a bar with every copy of each piece and where it is
- From Items, clicking ST Sets or Themed Sets melts them into one page, "Items and Sets"; a switch in its sets half flips between ST and themed sets, and a clicked set's pieces beat in the collection

**Keys:** 
- Clicking a mystery key opens a bar below it with every key it can give, the ones you hold highlighted
