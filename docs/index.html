Loot Gobbler

Your right hand for your RotMG adventures.

It keeps your collection in order, guards your UPE and PPE runs, suggests your loadout and enchants, follows your exaltations and dungeons, and brings your guild together. Written in Rust, so it won't bother your CPU.

How it works

It listens. That's all.

The Loot Gobbler reads what the game receives on your own PC, the way a network monitor does. It never sends, blocks or changes anything. Your records stay on your PC, and with a guild server you share them with the people you choose.

Every item, and where it sits

Game modes

A third eye on your UPE

Pick a game mode like UPE or PPE and the Loot Gobbler checks its rules as you play. It tracks every item the run farmed and every forge resource it may craft with, keeps its missions apart from the rest of your account, and tells you when and why a run was lost.

  1. VariantsUPE, PUPE, STPE, Shiny UPE, Divine PE, and PPE, with a guild and a no-pet version.
  2. What is allowedAt a glance: which gear counts and where it may come from.
  3. The rules, checked for youVault items, trades, shop and rewards are watched, no spreadsheet needed.
  4. FocusPut a run in focus and the whole app shows only what that run earned.
Focus

One run, the whole app

Put a game mode run in focus and the whole Loot Gobbler follows it. A gold line runs above the tabs with the run hanging from it, and every page shows that run alone: the items it farmed, the forge resources it may craft with, and the missions it can still do without breaking its rules.

1 2 3 4
  1. The focusThe run hangs from the gold line, always in sight. Click it to switch or leave.
  2. Only this runThe collection shows just the items this character farmed itself.
  3. What it may craft withThe forge resources it earned, kept apart from the rest of your account.
  4. Its own findsEvery drop of the run, newest first.

"For the focus": only the missions the run can still do on its own, without being disqualified.

Download

Get the Loot Gobbler

Free, and it tells you when a new version is out. Coming from the Java Loot Gobbler? Its first start offers to take over your records and settings.

Windows

LootGobbler.exe or the portable zip. Needs Npcap (default options).

Linux

.rpm or the portable .tar.gz. Sets itself up with one password prompt.

Optional. One Docker container for your guild's quests, leaderboards and sync.
